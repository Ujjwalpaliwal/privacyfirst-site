How It Works

Three modes. One guarantee.

Mode 01
Chat Checkpoint

Paste or type any prompt. PrivacyFirst detects PII inline, shows you exactly what will be sent, and restores placeholders in the AI reply.

Mode 02
Auto-protect

One toggle in Settings configures opencode, Claude Code, and Codex to route through the local proxy. Detection is invisible and automatic.

Mode 03
Live Proxy

Every outgoing API call passes through 127.0.0.1:4040. Secrets, credentials, and PII are replaced before upstream — restored before the tool sees the reply.

What We Claim

Honest by design

We claim
  • [+] Local detection — no text sent to any server for scanning
  • [+] Local vault — placeholder-to-original map never leaves your device
  • [+] Exactly what the AI sees — you review sanitized text before sending
  • [+] User-initiated only — nothing masked without your knowledge
We don't claim
  • [-] Full anonymization — inferred names are a best-effort guess, not a guarantee
  • [-] 100% detection rate — we reduce exposure, not eliminate risk
  • [-] Attachment/image scanning — text only in this version
  • [-] Protection against model memorization
Limitations

Known gaps

01
Names in unusual phrasing
Local NER catches bare names, but a name at the very start of a sentence ("Priya Menon called") can still slip through
02
Quasi-identifiers
Salary + location + age combined can re-identify without any individual PII
03
Attachments
PDFs, images, screenshots not scanned in current version
04
Non-text modalities
Audio, video not covered
05
Antigravity
Internal Google endpoints; no supported override (known gap)
