Use Cases

Who uses PrivacyFirst

Every AI workflow that touches real data is a use case.

Developers & Coding Agents
.env secret scrubbing via proxy

An AI coding agent like opencode reads your .env file. Without PrivacyFirst, the full DATABASE_URL, API keys, and secrets are sent to the model verbatim. With the proxy active, they're replaced before the request leaves your machine.

Before — sent to AI
DATABASE_URL=postgres://admin:p@ssw0rd@prod.db.internal:5432/app OPENAI_API_KEY=sk-proj-abc123xyz... STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_live_abc123xyz... REDIS_URL=redis://:password@cache.internal:6379
After — sanitized
DATABASE_URL=[SECRET_01] OPENAI_API_KEY=[SECRET_02] STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=[SECRET_03] REDIS_URL=[SECRET_04]
Tax & Finance Professionals
Tax prompt with Aadhaar, PAN, and salary

When asking an AI to help with ITR filing or tax advice, the prompt often contains the most sensitive data a person holds. PrivacyFirst masks it before the prompt ever reaches the model.

Before — sent to AI
My name is Rahul Sharma, PAN ABCDE1234F, Aadhaar 2345 6789 0124, email rahul@acme.in. My salary is 18,00,000 INR. Help me calculate my tax liability under the new regime.
After — sanitized
My name is [PERSON_01], PAN [PAN_01], Aadhaar [AADHAAR_01], email [EMAIL_01]. My salary is 18,00,000 INR. Help me calculate my tax liability under the new regime.
Amounts are kept — only identifiers are masked.
Customer Support Agents
Agent handling customer PII in ticket context

Support agents paste customer records into AI assistants for quick replies. Card numbers, emails, and phone numbers get masked before the AI processes the ticket.

Before — sent to AI
Customer: Priya Menon, priya@example.com Card ending: 4111 1111 1111 1111 Issue: Payment declined on order #ORD-8821
After — sanitized
Customer: [PERSON_01], [EMAIL_01] Card ending: [CARD_01] Issue: Payment declined on order #ORD-8821
Freelancers & Contract Work
Client data in project briefs

Freelancers often share client details with AI tools for drafting proposals, contracts, or reports. GST numbers, IFSCs, and email addresses are masked automatically.

Before — sent to AI
Client: Acme Corp GSTIN: 27ABCDE1234F1Z5 Bank: SBIN0001234 / Card: 4111 1111 1111 1111 Contact: cfo@acme.in, +91 98765 43210
After — sanitized
Client: Acme Corp GSTIN: [GSTIN_01] Bank: [IFSC_01] / Card: [CARD_01] Contact: [EMAIL_01], [PHONE_01]
Amounts are kept — only identifiers are masked.
Enterprise
Policy-enforced proxy for team AI usage

Education
Student submitting assignment with real personal data

Students asking AI for homework help sometimes include real contact info or ID numbers in their examples. PrivacyFirst acts as a safety net.

Before — sent to AI
Please check if my example is correct: Name: Rahul Kumar, Roll: 21BCE1234 Aadhaar: 2345 6789 0124
After — sanitized
Please check if my example is correct: Name: [PERSON_01], Roll: 21BCE1234 Aadhaar: [AADHAAR_01]
